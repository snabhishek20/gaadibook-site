Run your two-wheeler workshop
from your phone

GaadiBook is a simple Android app for bike and scooter service workshops in India. Job cards, bills, payments, stock, staff and the daily cashbook in one place, in your own language.

Coming soon on Google Play

Job cards in seconds

Scan the number plate, pick complaints with one tap or speak them, and take damage photos at intake.

Bills and payments

GST or simple bills, UPI or cash, part payments and old dues. Share the bill on WhatsApp.

Stock and purchases

Keep spare-parts stock and scan purchase bills to fill the parts list.

Staff and roles

Add mechanics and helpers. Each role sees only what it needs; wages and advances stay with the owner.

Daily cashbook

Today's income and expense at a glance, split by cash and UPI.

Sign in with Google, add staff with a QR code

Owners and staff sign in with their own Google account. No passwords or SMS codes to remember. The owner adds staff by showing a live QR code; nobody can join a workshop without the owner's permission.
