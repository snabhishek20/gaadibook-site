1. What we store

  • Your account: you sign in with your Google account. From Google we receive only your email address and name; we never see your Google password.
  • Your details: the name and mobile number you type in once after your first sign-in. The number is a contact number for your workshop. It is not used to sign in or to give access.
  • Your workshop: workshop name, address, phone number, UPI ID, GST details and bill terms.
  • Records you enter: customers and their phone numbers, vehicles and registration numbers, job cards, bills, payments, expenses, stock, staff details (including wages and advances) and vehicle intake / damage photos.
  • Joining a workshop: when an owner adds staff, we keep the join QR or code only as a one-way fingerprint (never the code itself), who used it and when. Failed join attempts are kept for one day to stop guessing.

Records are saved to our cloud, so the app needs internet to save. A copy stays on your phone so you can look up records quickly and see them when you are offline. The other phones of your workshop see the same records.

2. Who can see it

Only members of your workshop. A person becomes a member only when the owner (or a partner) lets them in: by showing them a live QR code, or by approving their request after they type a code. What each member sees depends on their role: for example, mechanics and helpers do not see payments, cash or wages. Each workshop's records are kept separate from every other workshop's on our servers. Workshop owners and partners see the name and mobile number of each staff member and of each person asking to join. We never sell your records or share them for advertising.

3. Services we use

  • Google Sign-In: signs you in. Google tells us your email address and name.
  • Supabase: cloud database, sign-in and photo storage.
  • Cloudflare R2: vehicle photo storage. Photos are private and shown only through short-lived links to members of the workshop.
  • Google Gemini: reads a purchase bill photo only when you use bill scan, to fill in the parts list.

Number plates and join QR codes are read on the phone, not on a server. All transfers are encrypted (HTTPS).

4. Camera, microphone and storage

  • Camera: scans number plates, purchase bills and workshop join QR codes, and takes vehicle damage photos.
  • Microphone: turns spoken complaints into text. Audio is not stored by GaadiBook.
  • Storage: used to share bills and estimates on WhatsApp.

Nothing is captured unless you start it.

5. Your customers' details

Customer names, phone numbers and vehicle numbers are entered by your workshop. Your workshop decides what to keep and is responsible for telling its customers. We store these details only to run the app for you.

6. How long we keep data

We keep your records while your account is active. When you delete your account, the records are removed from our live database at once, as described below. Copies in our providers' routine backups are overwritten on their normal backup cycle.

7. Deleting your account

🗑️ Delete your account and data

Option A — In the app:

Open GaadiBook → Account → Delete Account & Data. This is available to every member, owner or staff.

  • Your sign-in account is deleted from our cloud.
  • If you are the only owner of a workshop, that workshop is deleted for everyone, with all its customers, vehicles, job cards, bills, payments, expenses, staff records and photos.
  • If the workshop has another owner, or you are staff, only your membership is removed. The workshop's records stay with the remaining owner.
  • Your name and mobile number from Your details are deleted.
  • All GaadiBook data on the phone you used is wiped.

Deletion needs internet. If it fails, the app tells you and nothing is wiped, so you can try again.

Option B — By email (if you no longer have the app):

Send a request from the Google email you sign in with. We delete the account and data within 30 days.

Submit Deletion Request via Email

8. Your rights, questions and complaints

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data, and raise a complaint. Write to gaadibook.team@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our reply, you can approach the Data Protection Board of India.
